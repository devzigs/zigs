      The problem
      
Today you pick one: teamwork or privacy.

      
A shared wallet shows the world your members, your balance and every payment. A private wallet puts everything on one person's key. Neither is safe for a team.

    

    

      
1
No one acts alone
Set how many approvals a payment needs, like 2 of 3. No single person, phone or server can move your funds.

      
2
Nothing to trace
On chain, each payment looks like any other private Zcash transfer. No members, no rules, no balance.

      
3
Nobody in the middle
Your keys never leave your devices. Our server passes along encrypted messages it cannot open.

      How it works
      
Set up in minutes. Nobody ever holds the whole key.

      
Everything happens on your team's own devices.

    

    
    
      
  1. Invite your team
    Pick your rule, like 2 of 3, and share one link. The link carries your wallet's secret, so our server never sees it.
    2. 
      
  2. Create the key together
    Your devices build the wallet key as a group. Each person gets one piece. The full key never exists anywhere.
    3. 
      
  3. Review before you sign
    Your device reads the payment itself and shows you exactly where the money goes. Then you approve.
    4. 
      
  4. Send it privately
    Once enough people approve, the payment goes out as one ordinary private transaction.

      Security
      
Private from the world. Private from us too.

      
We built Zigs so that we cannot see your wallet, even if someone forces us to look.

Proven cryptography, no shortcuts

        

          
FROST group signatures (ZIP 312)
The Zcash standard for signing as a group. Each payment uses a fresh key, so nobody can link your payments.

          
Zcash private transactions
The same zero-knowledge proofs that protect every private Zcash payment. No new blockchain, no smart contracts.

          
End-to-end encryption
Your wallet's data is encrypted on your device before it reaches our server.

        

        
Zigs runs on testnet and has not been audited yet. Please do not use real funds.

      FAQ
      
Questions, answered.

    

    

      
Can Zigs take or freeze my funds?
No. We never hold any piece of your key, and we cannot read your wallet's data. At worst our server could slow messages down, and you can run your own.

      
What if someone loses their phone?
Your team can still pay as long as enough members remain to meet your rule. Each member can back up their piece of the key. Replacing a member is on the roadmap.

      
Has Zigs been audited?
Not yet. An independent audit comes before mainnet.

      
Can my co-signers see our payments?
Yes. Everyone in the wallet can check the balance and every payment for themselves. Nobody outside can.

      
When can I use real ZEC?
After the audit, once Zcash activates the network upgrade that Zigs is built on.
